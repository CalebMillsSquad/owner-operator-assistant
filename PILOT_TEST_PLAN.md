# Mills Trucking Pilot Test Plan

## Test Boundary

This invitation-only pilot contains fictional records only. Do not enter real driver names, load details, rates, receipts, documents, credentials, financial records, or regulated information. Screenshot upload is disabled until isolated object storage is approved.

## Tester Workflow

1. Open the protected preview URL supplied privately by the Founder.
2. Sign in with the invited tester email and a password delivered through a separate private channel.
3. Confirm the persistent `Mills Trucking Pilot - Test Data Only` banner and open **Pilot home**.
4. Review the Mills Trucking pilot profile and confirm the account shows the `TESTER` role.
5. Open **Assistant Command Center**. Review active loads, completed loads, expenses, maintenance reminders, document alerts, and next actions.
6. Open **Freight Intelligence**. Review both fictional opportunities and the equipment profile.
7. Open the negotiating Louisville-to-Atlanta opportunity. Review compatibility, total-mile profitability, recommendation reasons, and negotiation history.
8. Add a fictional counteroffer, change the opportunity status, and confirm the history updates.
9. Open the review-required Birmingham-to-Savannah opportunity and confirm the app explains the unresolved weight/schedule risk.
10. Create one new fictional opportunity. Never use a real broker, MC number, phone number, email, lane, rate, or load reference.
11. Convert an accepted fictional opportunity into a booked load and confirm the original opportunity remains available as history.
12. Review **Loads & Dispatch**, **Fuel Purchases**, **Expenses**, **Maintenance**, **Documents**, **Reports & Analytics**, and **Driver Assistant**.
13. At least once on desktop and once on a phone-size browser, open **Feedback**, choose a category, describe the issue, and submit it.
14. Sign out and confirm a private route redirects to sign-in.

## Feedback Questions

- Could you tell what needed attention first on the Command Center?
- Did Freight Intelligence make the total operational miles and estimated profit understandable?
- Was any recommendation unclear, misleading, or missing an important trucking consideration?
- Could you complete a negotiation and booked-load conversion without assistance?
- Which field or screen took the most effort?
- Did the fictional data feel realistic enough to evaluate the workflow without resembling a real business record?
- Did any page expose a route or action you should not have as a tester?
- What is the single most valuable change before a second tester is invited?

## Acceptance

The pilot passes when the invited tester can complete the workflow, submit feedback, sign out, and cannot access owner reset or any non-pilot data. The Founder reviews feedback before inviting another tester or considering production deployment.
